Ruling Out Early Distant Recurrence in Stage III Colon Cancer

A Simple Four-Variable Machine Learning Model
with External Validation

Kaohsiung Veterans General Hospital
E-DA Hospital · Taiwan
The Problem

Despite curative surgery, 20–30% of stage III colon cancer patients recur

<18 mo
Early distant recurrence (EDR)
Aggressive
Tumor biology signal
High risk
Poorer survival trajectory
Routine AJCC staging alone cannot reliably identify these patients early.
The Gap

Two unmet needs in the post-operative window

🧩
AJCC staging
Wide outcome variation within the same substage — especially stage IIIB
🧬
Molecular tools (ctDNA)
Powerful, but costly — not universally available in routine practice
Can routine pathology alone rule out early distant recurrence — before molecular testing or imaging?
